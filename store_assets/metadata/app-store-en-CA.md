## Description

League Hub gives sports organizations one clear place to keep their people informed and connected.

See the next game as soon as you open the app. Browse upcoming and completed schedules with team logos, scores, times, and venues. Catch important pinned announcements, continue team chats, open league policies, and find the right contact without digging through separate tools.

Built for leagues, associations, hubs, and teams:

• Upcoming games and recent results
• Team and league schedule views
• Pinned announcements and organized chats
• League policies and shared documents
• Organization contacts and role details
• Role-based access for owners, admins, managers, and staff
• Secure, organization-scoped information
• User reporting, blocking, and account deletion controls

League Hub is available to invited members of participating organizations. Schedule details remain subject to confirmation by your league.

## Review notes

League Hub is an organization-scoped league operations and communication app. Use the review account supplied in App Review Information. The account is a Staff / Team Coordinator in the isolated “League Hub Review League” organization and includes an upcoming game, a final result, pinned announcements, team chats, contacts, and a sample policy.

Suggested path: sign in → Home → Schedule → Chats → open Team Chat → Policy → Contacts.

Chat messages expose clearly labelled Report message, Report user, and Block user actions from the message actions menu. Settings → Privacy & Security includes the privacy policy, terms/community guidelines, support, and in-app account deletion.

No purchase is required. No production customer data is present in the review organization.
